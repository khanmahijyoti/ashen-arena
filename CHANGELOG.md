# 1.0.0-alpha.1

First public single-player alpha for Minecraft Java 26.3 / Fabric.

- Protected Ashen Hall with F8 placement and contextual E entry/return.
- Shift directional quickstep; Left Ctrl crouch and Right Ctrl sprint defaults.
- Ashen Katana with frontal guard, crafting, durability, and respawn replacement.
- Ashen Ogre with tracked grab/kick windups, readable cues, boss bar, defeat and rematch.
- Looping boss music on aggro, amplified during playback from the installed Minecraft asset. No game audio is redistributed.

Known limitations: multiplayer/LAN and dedicated servers unsupported; one hall per world; gate keys fixed; first-time construction can pause; no posture, timed deflection, stamina, or dodge invulnerability. Created blocks remain after uninstalling.

Validation: in-game boss integration covers native streaming sound resolution, battle volume boost and mute, aggro playback, looping flag, no combat restarts, defeat/death cleanup, rematch, guard, tracked grab/kick, late dodge, and katana respawn. Earlier arena, dodge, katana, guard, and respawn suites were exercised during development. Alpha testing does not establish compatibility with other mods.

Development disclosure: code and documentation were created primarily with AI assistance under the owner's direction. Screenshots are real gameplay captures.
